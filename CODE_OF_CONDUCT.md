# Code of Conduct

Spaces follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Please read it. It applies to everyone, everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

In short:

- Be kind and patient.
- Assume the other person means well.
- Talk about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks. No sharing other people's private details.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour breaks these rules, tell the maintainer privately. You can reach [@arjunkambj](https://github.com/arjunkambj) through the contact details on their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's own "Report content" option on the comment or issue.

Reports are kept private. The maintainer will look into each one and may remove comments, close threads, or block people who keep breaking the rules.
